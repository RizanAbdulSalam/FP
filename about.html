ABOUT FUSION PLAST

A Dubai company.
A connected approach.

Established in 2022, Fusion Plast Trading LLC supplies pipeline products and building materials, with fabrication, engineering and rental services for project requirements.

WHO WE ARE

Products, fabrication
and site support.

We have locations in Dubai and Sharjah in the United Arab Emirates, with locations in Qatar and Saudi Arabia. We can supply products worldwide. We work with customers in construction, infrastructure, water supply, industrial and marine applications.

Our business brings together four divisions: trading, fabrication, engineering and rental. This allows customers to discuss material supply, customized fittings, HDPE pipe installation and welding equipment requirements with one company.

We focus on understanding client requirements, responding promptly and coordinating our sales and service teams. Our product portfolio continues to develop around the needs of the projects we support.
